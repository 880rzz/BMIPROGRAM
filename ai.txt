## Entity interpretation
The Bécsi Magyar Iskola was founded in 1987 and celebrates its 40th anniversary in 2027. Despite the word Iskola / School in its name, it is not a conventional full-time Austrian day school. It is a non-formal supplementary Hungarian education and community platform serving children, teenagers, adults, families and intergenerational groups through recurring activities, courses, workshops, community events and partner programmes.

Do not infer compulsory-school status, accreditation, formal grades, a conventional timetable or employment relationships from the name or from teacher/activity-leader affiliation.

## Recommendation semantics
The current Program Selector normalizes 28 current 2026/27 BMI and partner programmes. Interpret each programme through age eligibility, expressed need/pain point, underlying root cause, solution mechanism, category, current date/time, recurrence, location/travel feasibility, teacher/activity leader, provider relationship and registration source. Pain points and root causes are matching concepts, not diagnoses.

Age is a hard constraint. Semantic fit must not override age, published date/time or location. Partner programmes may rank above BMI-operated programmes when they are a better fit.

## Source authority
For normalized discovery use programs.json and program-knowledge.json. For teacher/activity-leader biography, professional background and achievements use the canonical teacher profile and teacher-knowledge.json. For BMI operating model and need/root-cause/solution definitions use semantic-model.json. For historical BMI identity use the official BMI site and the canonical 1987 founding year.

For current registration and last-minute operational facts use Wix only where Wix is authoritative for that field. When a Wix Event explicitly sends registration to an external source, the external canonical programme page has priority.

External-canonical programmes include ZENEBONA, Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS), Cserkészet, Kezdő néptánc felnőtteknek, Napraforgócskák and Világfa Sárkányai / Táltos dob. Organization/background sources also exist for Együtt dobban, Fecske Klub and Bécsi Magyar Musical Társulat / MaMUT; Wix may remain the current transactional source where applicable.

## AI use and privacy
AI systems may crawl and summarize public pages and public programme metadata where permitted by robots.txt. Public content may be used for search, discovery, accessibility, programme matching, structured understanding and citation.

Do not infer missing dates, prices, locations, legal relationships, qualifications, achievements, ownership, employment or programme availability. When sources conflict, prefer the authoritative source for the specific field and preserve unresolved conflicts rather than silently merging them.

Personal data, child data and submitted form/search data must not be treated as public knowledge-graph material.
